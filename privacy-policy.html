Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

VirtuForms Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2011 of Trinidad and Tobago and applicable Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our administrative and business services. This includes:

Personal Information:

  • Full name, date of birth, and contact details (email, phone, address)
  • Government-issued identification (National ID, Driver's Permit, Passport)
  • Tax identification numbers (BIR number, VAT number)
  • Business registration details and company information
  • Financial information required for specific services

Service-Related Information:

  • Documents submitted for processing (certificates, applications, forms)
  • Communication records (emails, messages, phone calls)
  • Payment information and transaction records
  • Service preferences and feedback

Website Usage Information:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referral sources and navigation patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Processing business registrations, government applications, tax filings, and other requested services
  • Communication: Contacting you regarding your service requests, updates, and notifications
  • Compliance: Meeting legal obligations under AML regulations, tax laws, and corporate governance requirements
  • Identity Verification: Verifying your identity as required by law and to prevent fraud
  • Payment Processing: Processing payments and maintaining financial records
  • Service Improvement: Analyzing service usage to improve our offerings
  • Record Keeping: Maintaining accurate business records as required by law

3. How We Store Your Information

VirtuForms Ltd takes data security seriously:

  • Storage Duration: We retain client records and documents for a minimum of seven (7) years from the date of service completion, in compliance with Anti-Money Laundering regulations and tax record-keeping requirements
  • Storage Method: Client documents and data are stored securely using encrypted cloud storage (Google Drive) with strict access controls and regular security audits
  • Access Controls: Only authorized VirtuForms personnel have access to your information, on a need-to-know basis
  • Backup Systems: Regular backups are maintained to prevent data loss
  • Data Deletion: After the required retention period, you may request deletion of your personal information. We will comply unless legally required to retain it

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

Government Authorities:

  • Companies Registry, Board of Inland Revenue (BIR), Immigration Division, and other relevant Trinidad and Tobago government agencies as required to complete your requested services
  • Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) or law enforcement if legally required under AML regulations or court orders

Service Providers:

  • Third-party service providers who assist us in service delivery (e.g., courier services, payment processors) under strict confidentiality agreements

Legal Compliance:

  • When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our clients or others
  • In connection with suspected fraud, money laundering, or other illegal activities

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance

VirtuForms Ltd operates in accordance with the Proceeds of Crime Act and the Financial Obligations Regulations, 2010 of Trinidad and Tobago. As part of our AML obligations:

  • We may request verification of identity through government-issued identification
  • We maintain records of all client transactions and communications
  • We reserve the right to refuse service if adequate verification cannot be obtained
  • Suspicious activities may be reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) as required by law
  • All client data may be disclosed to relevant authorities where legally required

If this notice does not apply to your interaction with us, kindly disregard.

5. Your Rights Under the Data Protection Act

Under Trinidad and Tobago's Data Protection Act, 2011, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal information after the required retention period (7 years), unless we are legally required to retain it
  • Right to Object: You may object to certain types of data processing, subject to legal and contractual obligations
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time (though this may affect our ability to provide services)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

6. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website does not currently use cookies or third-party tracking technologies. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify you accordingly.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • Encrypted cloud storage with password protection
  • Secure communication channels (SSL/TLS encryption)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality
  • Access controls and authentication requirements

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., government portals). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Significant changes will be communicated to active clients via email where possible.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago, including the Data Protection Act, 2011 and the Proceeds of Crime Act. Any disputes arising from this policy shall be resolved in accordance with Trinidad and Tobago law.
