Required Documents

  • Computer Generated Birth Certificate
  • National ID
  • Deed Polls (if applicable)
  • Married Women must submit:
    • Marriage Certificate(s)
    • Divorce Decree(s) if applicable
    • Spouse's Death Certificate if applicable
  • If you hold citizenship of any country, other than your country of birth:
    • Documentary evidence from that country showing the date on which you acquired citizenship
    • Passport of that country
Important: We'll ask you about your citizenship status (Birth, Descent, Adoption, Registration, or Naturalisation) and verify you have the new computer-generated birth certificate before proceeding with your application.
