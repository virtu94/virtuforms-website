Frequently Asked Questions

A partnership in Trinidad and Tobago can have 2 or more partners. There's no strict maximum limit, but partnerships with many partners may want to consider registering as an LLC for better liability protection and management structure.
Each partner needs to provide: valid government-issued ID (National ID, Passport, or Driver's Permit), residential address with proof of address if not on ID, contact information (email and phone), occupation details, and an authorization letter appointing VirtuForms as your representative.
In a Partnership, all partners are personally liable for business debts and obligations. Your personal assets can be at risk. An LLC provides limited liability protection - the company is a separate legal entity, and your personal assets are generally protected from business liabilities. We recommend free consultation to discuss which structure suits your situation.

Your Annual Return is due on your company's anniversary date each year. For example, if your LLC was incorporated on March 15th, your Annual Return is due by March 15th every year. Late filing incurs a $300/month penalty, so we recommend filing at least 2 weeks before your deadline.
A BIR Number is your unique tax identification number that you obtain once. Tax Returns are annual filings you submit each year reporting your income and taxes. You need a BIR Number before you can file Tax Returns. This service gets you your initial BIR Number.
While not legally required for all businesses, a company seal adds professionalism to your documents and is often expected by banks and some business partners. Our digital seal can be used on electronic documents, contracts, and official correspondence. Physical stamps are available upon request.
