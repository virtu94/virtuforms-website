BIR Number Application

Fast and efficient assistance with obtaining your Board of Inland Revenue (BIR) number for tax purposes.

Required Documents

For Individuals (Employees):

  • Copy of National ID card (front and back) OR Passport OR Driver's Permit with Birth Certificate
  • Letter from employer indicating the effective date of employment and salary OR TD4s for prior years (not older than 2 years) OR Copy of Contract

For Individuals (Self Employed / Sole Traders):

  • Certificate of Registration (if applicable)
  • Income Statement(s) or Proof of Income
  • Copy of National ID card (front and back) OR Passport OR Driver's Permit with Birth Certificate
