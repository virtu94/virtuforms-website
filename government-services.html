Driver's Permit Renewal

Hassle-free renewal of your driver's permit with complete document preparation and submission.

Pricing Options

  • 5-Year Renewal: $835 (includes all government and delivery fees)
  • 10-Year Renewal: $1,335 (includes all government and delivery fees)

Required Documents

  • Physical Driver's Permit (required at our office)
  • Proof of Address (utility bill: Cable, Electricity, Water, or Internet)
  • Authorization letter if utility bill not in your name
  • Photo of applicant (within last 3 months, white background, no glasses)
  • Photo of signature (on blank white paper)
  • Authorization form for VirtuForms to represent you

Important Notes

  • If permit expired over 6 months, cannot be done online
  • Name and address changes must be done in office
  • Only Class 1 and 3 can be renewed online
Turnaround: 1-3 business days (review) + 1-5 days (delivery)
