Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about VirtuDrop.

Pricing & Fees
What are the delivery rates?

VirtuDrop charges based on zone coverage:

  • Primary Area Delivery — TTD $40 per package.
  • Extended Area Delivery — TTD $50 per package.
  • Remote or special routes are quoted before confirmation.
All prices are in Trinidad and Tobago Dollars (TTD).
Is there a pickup fee?

Pickup is free when collecting 5 or more packages. Pickups of 4 packages or fewer cost TTD $20.

Pickup is available in primary and extended zones. Parcels can also be dropped at our office for free.
What is Priority Same-Day delivery and how much does it cost?

Priority Same-Day is available only after discussion and quote. It is not a standard service and is subject to route availability on the day.

Priority Same-Day is not guaranteed. Contact us first to check availability and receive a quoted price.
Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes. We are flexible and willing to discuss a delivery rate based on your business's delivery volume and needs.

Book a free consultation with us to discuss a suitable rate.

Orders & Pickups
What is the daily order cut-off time?

Orders are reviewed, confirmed, and scheduled for the appropriate zone route.

Standard delivery takes 1–2 business days after the order is confirmed and scheduled.

Submit early so your order can be confirmed and placed into the next suitable route.
What happens if my order is submitted late?

Late or incomplete orders are placed into the next suitable route after they are reviewed and confirmed.

Standard delivery is 1–2 business days after confirmation and scheduling.

How does pickup work?

Pickups are conducted on scheduled days only — not on-demand or upon individual request. On your assigned pickup day, collection runs between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

  • Packages must be ready before collection.
  • Late or unavailable packages are moved to the next scheduled pickup day.
  • Pickup timing within the window is based on route efficiency — not by individual request.
  • Pickup is available in primary and extended zones.
  • Pickup is free when collecting 5 or more packages. Pickups of 4 packages or fewer cost TTD $20.
  • Parcels can be dropped at our office for free.
How do I schedule a pickup?

As a business client, you submit delivery orders through your client dashboard or by sharing your unique VirtuDrop delivery link with your customers. Once an order is reviewed and confirmed, it is scheduled for the appropriate pickup or delivery route.

You can also contact us directly via WhatsApp for assistance.

What package sizes and weights are accepted?

The following limits apply to all deliveries:

  • Maximum weight: 25 lbs per package
  • Oversized items: Not accepted

If you are unsure whether your item qualifies, contact us before submitting the order.

What items cannot be delivered?

We cannot deliver the following:

  • Hazardous materials (chemicals, flammables, explosives)
  • Perishable goods, including food and restaurant orders
  • Medicine requiring temperature control
  • Weapons or ammunition
  • Prohibited or illegal items
  • Items exceeding 25 lbs or oversized

If unsure, contact us before submitting the order.

Delivery
How long does delivery take?

Standard deliveries take 1–2 business days, depending on your zone's scheduled route.

Each zone has assigned delivery days. Your order is delivered on the next available scheduled day for your zone after pickup.

Specific delivery times are not accommodated. Deliveries run within the zone's assigned window for that day.
Can I request a specific delivery time?

No — specific delivery times are not available. All deliveries are conducted within the scheduled window for your zone on the assigned delivery day.

Deliveries follow a structured, zone-based schedule to ensure reliability and efficiency for all clients.

What if my package is not available for pickup on the scheduled day?

If your package is not ready during the 8:00 AM–10:00 AM pickup window on the scheduled day, it will be moved to the next scheduled pickup day.

Ensure packages are ready before collection to avoid delays.

How do I track my delivery?

You can track your delivery by order number or tracking link and see the latest status updates.

Visit our Track Delivery page to check the status of your order.

What if my package is damaged or lost?

Report any damage or loss within 24 hours of delivery by contacting our support team.

Provide photos of the damage and your delivery confirmation. We will investigate and work with you to resolve the issue.

Business Clients
Who can use VirtuDrop?

VirtuDrop is designed for business clients including:

  • E-commerce stores and online retailers
  • Subscription box services
  • Courier and logistics agencies
  • Wholesale and distribution companies
  • Any business sending packages in Trinidad

Contact us to set up your business account.

How do I sign up as a business client?

To set up a business account with VirtuDrop:

  1. Fill out the business inquiry form.
  2. Our team will review your information and contact you.
  3. Once approved, you'll receive login credentials and a unique delivery link.
  4. Start submitting orders through your dashboard or share your link with customers.
Do you provide customer support?

Yes — we provide full support to all business clients. Reach us via:

  • WhatsApp: Direct messaging and support
  • Email: For detailed inquiries
  • Dashboard: Real-time order tracking and history
  • Phone: For urgent matters

Contact us for support details.

Payments & COD
How do I pay for deliveries?

VirtuDrop offers flexible payment options:

  • Monthly Invoicing: Business clients are invoiced monthly for all deliveries.
  • Monthly Plans: Subscribe to Bronze, Silver, or Gold plans for locked-in rates.
  • Per-Delivery Billing: Pay-as-you-go rates for single shipments.

Contact us to discuss which option works best for your business.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes — we can handle Cash on Delivery payments for your customers. When a customer chooses COD:

  • Payment is collected upon delivery.
  • Online transfer remittance is processed in the evening after confirmed delivery.
  • Cash is returned on your next pickup or through our office.
  • VirtuDrop maintains a full transaction record for your accounting.
COD services available for all delivery zones. Ask us about our COD service fees.
