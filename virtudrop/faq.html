Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about VirtuDrop.

Pricing & Fees
What are the delivery rates?

VirtuDrop charges based on zone coverage:

  • Same Zone Delivery — TTD $40 per package. Pickup and drop-off are within the same delivery zone.
  • Cross Zone Delivery — TTD $50 per package. Pickup and drop-off are in different zones.
All prices are in Trinidad and Tobago Dollars (TTD).
Is there a pickup fee?

Yes — pickup fees apply based on the number of packages ready for collection per visit:

  • 3 or more packages — Free pickup
  • Under 3 packages — TTD $20 collection fee
Tip: If you have fewer than 3 packages, consider batching orders to the same pickup to avoid the fee.
What is Priority Same-Day delivery and how much does it cost?

Priority Same-Day is an urgent delivery option for situations where a scheduled collection was missed and the item needs to be delivered before the next cycle.

It is not a standard service and is subject to route availability on the day.

Priority Same-Day is not guaranteed. Contact us before 8:00 AM to check availability and receive a quoted price.
Do you offer monthly plans or volume discounts?

Yes — we offer three recurring monthly logistics plans for consistent business clients:

  • Bronze — 20 deliveries/month at $38 per drop (TTD $760/month)
  • Silver — 40 deliveries/month at $36 per drop (TTD $1,440/month)
  • Gold — 75 deliveries/month at $34 per drop (TTD $2,550/month)

We also offer a Structured Weekly Plan at TTD $225/week for up to 6 deliveries in one designated zone. Visit our Pricing page for full details.

Orders & Pickups
What is the daily order cut-off time?

All orders must be submitted by 8:00 PM daily to be included in the next delivery cycle.

Orders submitted after 8:00 PM are automatically scheduled for the following delivery day cycle.

Submit early to ensure your order is picked up and delivered as quickly as possible.
What happens if my order is submitted after 8:00 PM?

Any order submitted after the 8:00 PM cut-off is automatically moved to the next available delivery cycle. It will not be processed the same night.

You will be notified and your order will be queued for the next scheduled pickup window.

How does pickup work?

Pickups are conducted on scheduled days only — not on-demand or upon individual request. On your assigned pickup day, collection runs between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

Packages must be ready before collection. Late or unavailable packages are moved to the next scheduled pickup day. Pickup timing within the window is based on route efficiency — not individual requests.
How do I schedule a pickup?

As a business client, you submit delivery orders through your client dashboard or by sharing your unique VirtuDrop delivery link with your customers. Once an order is submitted before the 8:00 PM cut-off, it is automatically queued for the next pickup window.

You can also contact us directly via WhatsApp for assistance.

What package sizes and weights are accepted?

The following limits apply to all deliveries:

  • Maximum weight: 25 lbs per package
  • Oversized items: Not accepted

If you are unsure whether your item qualifies, contact us before submitting the order.

What items cannot be delivered?

We cannot deliver the following:

  • Illegal goods or substances
  • Hazardous or flammable materials
  • Perishable food items without proper packaging
  • Live animals or plants
  • Firearms, weapons, or ammunition
  • Items exceeding 25 lbs
  • Oversized items that cannot be handled safely
Delivery
What is the delivery timeline?

Standard deliveries are completed within 1–2 days from the date of pickup. However, delivery is not on-demand — it runs on your zone's scheduled delivery day.

After your package is picked up, it will be delivered on the next available scheduled delivery day for your zone. The exact day depends on your zone's assigned schedule and when in the cycle your order was received.

Specific delivery times within a scheduled day are not accommodated. All deliveries run within the zone's assigned window for that day.
How does the customer get notified on delivery day?

Customers are contacted on the morning of their delivery day to confirm the delivery is on the way. This is done via phone call or WhatsApp message.

The client is also notified once the delivery is completed.

How is proof of delivery confirmed?

Delivery confirmation is sent via WhatsApp message to the client upon successful completion of each delivery. This serves as your official proof of delivery.

What happens if a delivery attempt fails?

One delivery attempt is made per order. If the attempt fails:

  • The delivery is marked as a failed attempt
  • The client is notified immediately
  • A new delivery fee applies for any redelivery attempt
If the customer confirmed the delivery when contacted but then did not collect — the delivery fee still applies. The client is responsible for determining whether they or the customer absorbs this cost.
What if the recipient is unavailable?

The driver will attempt to contact the recipient by phone. If no contact is made, the delivery is marked as failed.

Redelivery requires a new delivery fee. The client decides whether to reattempt and who covers the cost.

What if a customer asks to reschedule?

Rescheduled deliveries fall into the next available delivery cycle for that zone. There is no additional charge for rescheduling as long as the package has not yet been dispatched for delivery.

Once a driver is out for delivery, the standard failed delivery and redelivery policy applies.

What if a customer cancels before the driver attempts delivery?

If the customer cancels before a delivery attempt is made, no delivery fee is charged.

Please notify us as early as possible so we can update the driver's route accordingly.

How are returns handled?

Returns are processed only upon client request. A return delivery is treated as a full delivery and is charged at the standard delivery rate for the applicable zone.

Returns are conducted on scheduled delivery days, not on-demand. The client determines whether they or the customer is responsible for the return delivery cost.

What if a customer claims they did not receive the item?

If proof of delivery has been recorded and sent to the client via WhatsApp, the delivery is considered complete.

The client bears responsibility for resolving the dispute with the customer. VirtuDrop's responsibility ends once proof of delivery is provided.

Do you deliver on holidays?

Our goal is to maintain reliable service throughout the week including weekends.

  • Deliveries may be available on certain public holidays depending on operational capacity
  • Clients receive advance notice of any closures or schedule changes on public holidays
Business Clients
How does the unique client delivery link work?

Every registered business client receives their own unique VirtuDrop delivery link — for example: virtuforms.net/virtudrop/delivery-form.html?business=your-business-name

When a customer opens your link:

  • The form is automatically tied to your business
  • The customer fills in their name, phone number, delivery address and payment details
  • The order is automatically attached to your account
You simply share your link with customers — no manual order entry needed.
How can a customer track their delivery?

Customers have two ways to track their delivery:

  • Tracking link — after submitting a delivery request, the customer receives a unique tracking link via WhatsApp. Clicking it shows their live order status with no login required.
  • Phone + Order ID — if they lose the link, they can visit the Track Delivery page and enter their phone number and order ID.
What information do I need to provide when submitting an order?

Complete delivery details must be provided before pickup, including:

  • Pickup location
  • Customer full name
  • Customer contact number
  • Customer drop-off location (Google Maps pin or clear street address)
  • Payment status — whether the customer is paying cash on delivery, paid online for the item only, or paid online for both item and delivery
  • Any special requirements for the item
Payments & COD
What payment methods do you accept for deliveries (COD)?

VirtuDrop collects on behalf of clients via:

  • Cash — collected at the point of delivery
  • LINX — card payment collected at the point of delivery
When will I receive my remittance?

Remittance timelines depend on how the customer paid:

  • Online payments — remitted the same evening after delivery completion via online transfer
  • Cash payments — returned on collection day on a weekly basis
You can view your remittance records directly in your client dashboard, including payment method and status.
