What You Get

Everything a business client needs

A structured delivery system designed around the way your business actually works.

🔗

Unique Delivery Links

Create a customer-specific delivery link from your dashboard for each item or package. Customers complete their delivery details, then you review and submit the final order.

📊

Client Dashboard

A dedicated dashboard to submit orders manually, view pending and completed deliveries, track COD records, and download remittance history filtered by day, week or month.

💰

COD & Remittance

Drivers collect cash or LINX on your behalf at the point of delivery. Online remittance is transferred in the evening after confirmed delivery. Cash is returned on your next pickup or through our office.

📍

Auto Zone Detection

When a customer submits their delivery address or Google Maps pin, the system automatically detects their delivery zone and calculates the applicable rate.

📲

Customer Tracking

Customers can use their order number or tracking link to see the latest delivery status updates — no account needed.

✅

WhatsApp Confirmation

You receive a WhatsApp delivery confirmation once every order is completed. That confirmation serves as your official proof of delivery.
