About VirtuDrop

A structured, zone-based delivery service built exclusively for business clients across Trinidad.

Who we are

VirtuDrop is a delivery service operated by VirtuForms Ltd, built from the ground up to serve business clients who need reliable, scheduled delivery without the unpredictability of ad-hoc courier services.

We are not a general public courier. Every client is a business, and every delivery follows a structured process — from order submission to pickup to delivery confirmation.

Our zone-based model keeps things predictable. You know when deliveries run, what they cost, and what to expect. No surprises.

How we operate

Every business client receives a unique VirtuDrop delivery link. When you share that link with a customer, they fill in their name, phone number, delivery address and payment details — and the order is automatically attached to your account.

Our system detects the delivery zone from the address or Google Maps pin provided, assigns the appropriate rate, and queues the order for the next scheduled pickup and delivery cycle.

Admin reviews each order before confirming, ensuring accuracy before a driver is assigned. You and your customer can both track the delivery status in real time — no login required for customers.

Remittance is handled promptly — online payments are transferred the same evening, and cash collected on delivery is returned weekly.

Our Services

What we offer

Two core delivery tiers designed around the needs of business clients.

📦

Standard Delivery

Zone-based scheduled delivery for regular business orders. Same zone deliveries are TTD $40 per package. Cross zone deliveries are TTD $50. Delivered within 1–2 cycles from pickup based on your assigned zone schedule. Orders must be submitted by 8:00 PM daily.

⚡

Priority Same-Day

An urgent delivery option for situations where a scheduled collection was missed and the item needs to be handled before the next cycle. Subject to route availability — not guaranteed. Contact us to confirm whether it can be accommodated and to receive a quoted price.
