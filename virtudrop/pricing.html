Pickup Structure

How Pickups Work

Packages are collected on your scheduled pickup day within the morning window.

Pickup Schedule

Pickup is conducted on scheduled days only — not on-demand or upon request.
The pickup window runs between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM on your assigned collection day.
Packages must be ready before collection. Late or unavailable packages are moved to the next scheduled pickup day.
Pickup timing within the window is determined by route efficiency — not by individual request.
Delivery Schedule

When Will My Order Be Delivered?

Deliveries are zone-based and run on scheduled days — not on-demand.

Delivery Timeline

Deliveries are completed within 1–2 days from the date of pickup, subject to your zone's scheduled delivery day.
Each zone has assigned delivery days. Your order is delivered on the next available scheduled day for your zone after pickup.
Specific delivery times within a scheduled day are not accommodated. All deliveries run within the zone's assigned window for that day.
Orders submitted after the 8:00 PM cut-off are queued for the following delivery cycle and will be picked up on the next scheduled pickup day.
Policies

Operational Policies

Structured rules that keep deliveries consistent and reliable for all business clients.

🔄

Failed Delivery & Redelivery

One delivery attempt is made per order. If the customer confirms and then does not collect, the delivery fee still applies. Redelivery requires a new delivery fee. The client determines who absorbs the cost.

✅

Proof of Delivery

Delivery confirmation is sent via WhatsApp message upon successful completion of each delivery.

💳

Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available. Payments can be collected via cash or LINX on behalf of the client at the point of delivery.

💸

Remittance

Online payments are remitted the same evening after delivery completion. Cash payments are returned on a weekly basis.

📦

Package Guidelines

No large or oversized items are accepted. Maximum weight is 25 lbs per package. Items outside these limits will not be collected.

Note: All prices are in Trinidad and Tobago Dollars (TTD). Routes are planned in advance and cannot be adjusted for last-minute changes. VirtuDrop reserves the right to reschedule deliveries, decline requests, or adjust routes for operational efficiency.
