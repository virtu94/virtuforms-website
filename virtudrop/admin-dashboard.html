Managed Accounts

Businesses whose deliveries VirtuDrop manages directly (bulk import, remittance, reports) instead of the business having its own login — orders arrive through a broker such as Middleman Marketing on behalf of the businesses they represent.

Select a managed account to see its businesses and get started.
Add Order Manually
CSV Batch Import
Required columns: name, contact, item, area, business, external_order_number, total. Address is optional (combined with area for the delivery location). Business must match one of this managed account's businesses (e.g. "C&H Electronics" or "Scents") — a single file can mix rows for both. external_order_number is Middleman's own order number (not VirtuDrop's) and is required — it's used later for remittance. Enter "Exchange" in the total column for a warranty/item exchange with no item price attached, or "Refund" when Middleman supplies cash for the driver to hand the customer while collecting the item back — either way VirtuDrop's flat fee is deducted from that business's remittance instead of being charged to the customer. Order number, price, and notes are filled in automatically — do not include a notes column.
